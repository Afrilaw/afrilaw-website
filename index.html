Environmental harm, found and verified, for the lawyers who can act on it

AfriLaw uses AI to spot signs of environmental harm in Zimbabwe, from mining, forestry, wildlife and water damage, and our analysts check every signal before it reaches a law firm.

Request a demo

The problem

Communities harmed by pollution, unlawful mining or damaged water sources rarely reach court. The evidence is scattered, the law is hard to navigate, and the firms that could bring a class action often never hear about the harm.

How it works

1
DetectAI scans public information for signs of environmental harm and flags the strongest signals.
2
VerifyAn AfriLaw analyst reviews each signal before it goes any further. Nothing reaches a firm unchecked.
3
ResearchEach matter is linked to the Zimbabwean law that may apply, such as the Environmental Management Act and the Class Actions Act, and every reference links to its published source on ZimLII, so you can check it yourself.
4
ConnectVerified matters go to plaintiff and public-interest law firms, who decide whether to act.

Who it's for

Plaintiff and public-interest law firmsA pipeline of verified environmental matters, with the relevant law already linked.
Environmental and community organisationsA way for evidence of harm to reach lawyers who can act on it.
Litigation and impact fundersAccess-to-justice cases, screened before they reach you.

Our approach

  • AI-detected, analyst-verified: a person checks every matter.
  • Every legal reference links to the published source, so you can check it yourself.
  • Built for Zimbabwean law first.
  • We don't use personal data in demos, and we don't train AI models on client information.

Where we are
